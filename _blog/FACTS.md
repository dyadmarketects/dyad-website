## Field notes (unused)
First-hand input from Vince: observations, mistakes he sees clients make, how he actually does something, opinions, photos (put image files in `images/` and name them here). Optional: posts use one when it fits. Add a line anytime, even a rough one. Examples of useful notes:
- Most AI logos I see fall apart when edits need to be made. Many business owners get very frustrated in the "prompt and pray" process trying to have AI make the changes to their logo, or flyer for them even when the ask is simple. AI sometimes changes other things you don't want changed, and by the time you get close, got forbit you havn't already run out of tokens, you spent so much time equity that it would've been cheaper to have just hired a professional graphic designer. Some issues with AI generated logos is they may look beautiful at first but when it comes to actually fucntionability for your brand they fall flat. AI will provide you with a "flattened" image that when scaled up you will find that it's pixelated. Throw it at AI to Upscale it and it gets cloudy and distorted. scale it down and a logo that's too complex won't be legible. Also, AI generated logos may spit out designs with complex gradients and/or lines that are too thin for embroidery. That counts you out of some sleek polos and caps to rep your brand in the streets. I see alot of companies having to resort to screen printing to their designs because they can't be embroidered - which cheapens a luxury brand look. A professional graphic designer will provide you with a logo and designs that are versatile and perform for all your specific brand needs and can edit to fit exactly what you're asking for. No more frustration and wasted time equity on prompt and pray. 
- Credibility in AI generated logos and images. AI is becoming so widely used because a child with no experience can use it. The caveat is that your customer knows what minimal effort it takes to make some basic AI design and it's all noticably AI generated. That tells the customer, you're not invested in their experience from the jump. What can they infer about your product or service - the same thing. Alot of brands are shooting themselves in the foot by not investing in a professional for designs that yeild better customer connections, engagement, relationships and ultimately sales. AI generated food flyers is a great example of that. I had a client who before onboarding me to manage his social media, lost a customer over an ai generated image of a hamburger for his restaurant. The customersaid publically, "I will never eat at a restaurant that uses AI." They commented this publically so there's no telling how much potential business was lost before I was able to go in and deploy an apology campaign. If your product is good the TAKE A PICTURE OF IT. If it's not good enough for a photo then you need to go back to the drawing board not Chat GPT.
- Marketing Strategy is commonly mistaken as a luxury instead of a necessity - however it is a part of every facet of your business and needs to be structurally sound for more rapid and effective growth. This isn't the fancy clothes on top of your company - it's the entire skeletal structure beneath and the determining factor for how those clothes look. From establishing your market (competitive analysis, USP identification, and target market) to establishing your customer-facing messaging, core values key milestones for your operational process to deliver what you promise for increased credibility, referrals, and growth.
