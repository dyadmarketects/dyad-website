- 2. Strategy development: scope of work and recommendations, marketing strategy, forecasted timeline and investment schedule.
- 3. Execution. 4. Review & implementation (meet with your Marketect to finalize drafts). 5. Communication before, during and after.
- Existing brands: "audit what's there, identify what's holding it back, and build a plan to refine, not necessarily replace, what you've already built."
