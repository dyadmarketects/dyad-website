# Monthly notes

Optional. Put your own commentary for a month in `_seo/notes/YYYY-MM.md` (insights, what you'll do next).
It is printed as written under "Notes and next steps" in that month's report. The report itself never
adds interpretation: it only shows numbers from GA4, Search Console and your gbp file.
Supports paragraphs, `- ` bullets, `**bold**` and `## headings`.
