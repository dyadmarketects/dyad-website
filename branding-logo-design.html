FAQ

Branding & Logo Design questions

Can you refresh my existing logo instead of starting over?

Yes. That is a big part of what we do. We audit what is there, identify what is holding it back, and refine rather than replace what you have already built. For Gorman Industries, a company serving New Mexico since 1950, that meant realignment, not reinvention.

What do I get at the end of a branding project?

It depends on your goals, and the exact deliverables are spelled out in your proposal before work begins. Projects can include logo design, a brand identity system, business cards, email signatures, signage and other graphic design.

Can you work from a logo concept made with AI?

Yes. For The Rusty Nail Saloon we took an AI generated concept and rebuilt it by hand in Illustrator so it was scalable, embroidery ready and actually usable. AI can be a starting point, not a finish line.
