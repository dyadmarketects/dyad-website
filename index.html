Our Process

1
Initial Consultation — BRAND$TORM™
A collaborative, preliminary deep-dive brainstorm and discovery session designed to systematically uncover the purpose, long-term vision, and marketing strategy for your brand.
2
Strategy Development
A scope of work and recommendations are developed from our session discoveries. You'll receive a marketing strategy, a forecasted timeline, and an investment schedule.
3
Execution
We bring the strategy to life with precision. With your approval, our team handles every aspect — from creative design to technical execution — keeping you informed when it matters, and leaving you be when it doesn't. Your desired level of involvement is set in the initial BRAND$TORM™.
4
Review & Implementation
You meet with your Marketect to finalize drafts. Insights are gathered and implemented ahead of final launch.
5
Communication
We maintain open communication before, during, and after the project — so nothing gets lost, and nothing launches as a surprise.
About Vincent King, Founder of DYAD

Duality Wasn't a Strategy — it Was How I Grew Up.

Vincent Inkosi King is the founder of DYAD Marketects, an Albuquerque, New Mexico branding, web design and content studio. With 20+ years in immersive media, 3D animation, graphic design and digital marketing, he pairs creative work with data-driven strategy. DYAD stands for Duality Yielding Agile Development: two forces, one system.

'90

Two worlds, one house

Born in 1990 and raised by an electrical engineer father and an artist mother who homeschooled us to think critically. Logic and creativity were never separate rooms in that house.

'02

Animating a planetarium dome at 12

I took my passion for graphic design, immersive media, and 2D/3D animation and started applying it on purpose. I joined the UNM LodeStar Astronomy Production Team as a 3D model designer and animator on Arrow to the Sun, a full dome 360 production for the New Mexico Natural History Museum planetarium, based on Gerald McDermott's Caldecott Medal winning book.

'03

First of its kind at UNM

At 13, I enrolled as a student and teaching assistant in a college level 3D animation and immersive media full dome course at the University of New Mexico, the first one ever offered there.

'09

Maya Skies, then architecture

Graduated from Albuquerque Academy, then took my senior project to the UNM ARTS Lab as a 3D modeler and animator on the full dome production Tales of the Maya Skies, where my animations were shown worldwide. After that I studied architecture at UNM.

'10

The DYAD within, validated

At the ARTS Lab I worked on full dome animation, CNC routing, and a 360 degree immersive skateboarding video game. It aired on Discovery Channel Australia, was unveiled at Mesa del Sol in front of the governor, and made Albuquerque Business First. The rebel and the nerd, creating in unison.

'11

Architecture major, film intern

Earned a spot in the exclusive UNM IFDM (Interdisciplinary Film and Digital Media) summer internship, even as an architecture major. I created a full dome short film with 360 HDR photography, stitching, After Effects, and Autodesk Maya.

'11

Giving back

Lead Instructor for Summer Majik, a STEM initiative of the UNM Summer Enrichment camp run by the late Debra Johns, a longtime Albuquerque educator. Across two semesters of an intensive two week program, I taught middle school students production basics with professional software: After Effects animation, Photoshop, GarageBand audio, and Chroma Key compositing. They researched influential African American figures in New Mexico history and animated their stories with original audio and voiceover, then presented to families and faculty.

'13

Recalibrating into marketing

Architecture tickled the fascination but didn't scratch the itch, so I recalibrated. I joined ExploreMedia360, a New Mexico marketing agency start-up focused on graphic design, web design, and 360 tours in partnership with Google Maps.

'17

Architecture meets marketing

After the business sold, I went back to school and studied both disciplines side by side, graduating Phi Theta Kappa in Digital Marketing & Communications. Structure and story, same toolkit.

'19

Building marketing at DSI

Started as a Graphic Designer, grew into Marketing Manager & Senior Graphic Designer, and built and now lead an entire marketing department running campaigns across web, print, social, email, events, and video for six plus years.

'22

Rebel meets professional

Stopped treating the skateboarder and the strategist as different people. Channeled the same resourcefulness into client work, and it started showing.

'26

DYAD Marketects is born

Two forces, one system. A studio built on the belief that creativity and analytics were never actually competing, they just needed the right bridge. Based in Albuquerque, New Mexico.

FAQ

Got Any Questions?

Why a dual-discipline studio instead of a specialist?+

Most agencies pick a lane — pure creative, or pure analytics. DYAD treats them as one system, so your brand story and your KPIs are built together from day one instead of getting reconciled after the fact.

Already have a brand that needs work?+

That's a big part of what I do. I'll audit what's there, identify what's holding it back, and build a plan to refine — not necessarily replace — what you've already built.

What's the process from start to launch?+

It starts with the BRAND$TORM™ — a conversation about your goals and timeline. From there I scope the project, build in stages, and share progress as I go so feedback stays easy and nothing drifts.

Do you handle design, or only strategy?+

Both — that's the whole premise of DYAD. Brand identity, web design, and content sit right alongside the growth and analytics work, under one roof.

How involved do I need to be?+

As involved as you want. Your desired level of involvement gets identified in the initial BRAND$TORM™ — we'll keep you informed when it matters, and leave you be when it doesn't.
