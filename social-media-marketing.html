Our process

How social media management works

Every social media engagement starts with a BRAND$TORM™ session about your audience and goals, then moves through five clear steps.

  1. BRAND$TORM™ consultation

    A collaborative discovery session to uncover the purpose, long term vision and marketing strategy for your brand.

  2. Strategy development

    A scope of work, recommendations, a forecasted timeline and an investment schedule built from our session.

  3. Execution

    We bring the strategy to life, keeping you informed when it matters and leaving you be when it doesn’t.

  4. Review & implementation

    You meet with your Marketect to finalize drafts, and insights are implemented ahead of launch.

  5. Communication

    Open communication before, during and after the project, so nothing launches as a surprise.
FAQ

Social Media & Content Marketing questions

Can you manage our social media for us?

Yes. For Houser Raspberry Ranch we created two years of consistent, on brand content that grew the profile from 0 to 2.7K followers.

Do you also design flyers, posters and banners?

Yes. Print and digital work together. We have created posters, flyer designs and digital marketing for community events like a fall festival and car show in Mora.

Can you help us get press coverage?

Yes. For the Valley of the Mills Association, media pitches, event branding and a custom visitor app turned a two day tour into statewide coverage, including a KRQE feature.

Do you offer email marketing?

Yes. Email marketing is part of our social media and content work, along with social media management, content creation, flyers and banners.
