Our process

How a video project works

Every video and photo project starts with a BRAND$TORM™ session about the story you want to tell, then moves through five clear steps.

  1. BRAND$TORM™ consultation

    A collaborative discovery session to uncover the purpose, long term vision and marketing strategy for your brand.

  2. Strategy development

    A scope of work, recommendations, a forecasted timeline and an investment schedule built from our session.

  3. Execution

    We bring the strategy to life, keeping you informed when it matters and leaving you be when it doesn’t.

  4. Review & implementation

    You meet with your Marketect to finalize drafts, and insights are implemented ahead of launch.

  5. Communication

    Open communication before, during and after the project, so nothing launches as a surprise.
FAQ

Video & Photo Production questions

What kinds of video do you produce?

Brand films, promotional content, social content, show graphics for venue video boards, and immersive media. For Chesterton Academy of Albuquerque we produced a five minute brand film in two weeks.

Can one video be used in more than one place?

Yes, and we plan for it. The Chesterton Academy film was built for reuse across fundraising, social media, the website and onboarding.

Do you create content for LED screens and digital signage?

Yes. We created show graphics for the in-house video boards at the Rio Rancho Events Center, and trained Global Give-A-Book’s team to use their LED posters.

Do you also offer photography?

Yes. Product and brand photography is part of our video and photo work, along with brand films, digital signage and immersive media.
